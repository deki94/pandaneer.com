Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy describes how Pandaneer Bot, an internal tool used by the creator of the Pandaneer channels (pandaneer.com), handles data. Pandaneer Bot is not offered to the public. It is used only by the owner of the Pandaneer YouTube channel, the @pandaneer.tech Instagram account, the Pandaneer Facebook Page and the Pandaneer TikTok account to publish and schedule their own videos and to read their own statistics.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google) data: with the owner's consent, the app uploads videos to the owner's own channel, updates their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails and publish times, lists the channel's own uploads, and reads channel and video statistics (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API).
  • Instagram, Facebook and TikTok data: the app publishes videos to the owner's own accounts and reads their basic profile information and post statistics.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the owner's content and to create statistics reports for the owner.
  • Access tokens and statistics are stored only locally on the owner's own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by Pandaneer and are not shared with third parties.
  • The app does not collect data about viewers or other users, does not use data for advertising, and does not sell data.

Google API Services

Pandaneer Bot uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy. Pandaneer Bot's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings (and in the settings of Instagram, Facebook and TikTok). Deleting the app's local "tokens" and "statistiky" folders removes all stored data.
